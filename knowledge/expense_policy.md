# Business Expense Policy

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable
business expenses incurred while performing their work.

Business expenses must be submitted for reimbursement
within 30 days of the transaction.

Employees must provide a receipt for expenses of
50 USD or more.

Expenses above 500 USD require approval from the
employee's manager before the purchase is made.

Personal expenses are not eligible for reimbursement.

When traveling for business, employees should use
reasonable transportation and accommodation options.
