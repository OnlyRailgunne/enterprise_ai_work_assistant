# Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave
per calendar year.

Annual leave requests should normally be submitted at
least five business days in advance.

Employees should coordinate planned leave with their
manager and team members.

Urgent leave may be requested with shorter notice when
unexpected circumstances occur.

Unused annual leave may be carried over to the following
year, subject to company policy and applicable regulations.

Employees should record approved leave in the company's
leave management system.
